# specs/

Condensed, public-safe renderings of adopted specs cleared for this repo.

See the [umbrella README](../README.md) for the broader thesis. Sibling
subtrees: [`claude-md/`](../claude-md/), [`skills/`](../skills/),
[`memory/`](../memory/), [`memsearch/`](../memsearch/).

## What lives here

A spec is the non-trivial unit of work in this practice — propose, debate,
implement, finalize (umbrella README, theme 3). The private originals carry
implementation detail, internal paths, and per-session bookkeeping that does not
belong on a public surface. What ships here is a condensed rendering: the
design, the constraints, the tradeoffs, and the rejected alternatives, with the
private scaffolding removed.

Inclusion is per-spec and deliberate. A spec appears here only when its
engineering content stands on its own without the private tree around it. The
absence of a spec is not a backlog signal.

## Refresh

Re-rendered when the source spec changes; not on a clock.
